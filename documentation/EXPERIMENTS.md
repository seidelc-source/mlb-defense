- **Calibration data (ONE fit)**: robust nonlinear least squares of `hit_distance_sc ≈ s·‖(hc_x, hc_y) − (x0, y0)‖` over **air balls only** (fly/line/popup, ~628k pairs with both fields; soft_l1 loss, distance ≥ 30 ft, random 200k subsample seed 42). **Grounders EXCLUDED with cause** (discovered pre-design, recorded): their mean `hit_distance_sc` is 33 ft — it measures contact/first-bounce, while grounder `hc` marks the *fielded* location; the pair is semantically inconsistent. - **Hypothesis**: The engine's implied per-player out-value — Δ calibrated P(out|BIP) credited to a fielder's sprint-speed-driven reach vs a league-average fielder at the same position — tracks actual Statcast OAA across players: positive rank correlation, and implied magnitudes on the OAA scale.

- **Intervention/quantity (ONE)**: per (player, season, position) with OAA + sprint speed and **innings ≥ 300**: implied_delta_per_BIP = `calibrated_out_probability`(league landing density, standard alignment, calibrator v1, league ground share) with the target position's reach at the player's sprint speed MINUS the same with league-average (per-position mean) sprint speed; all other six fielders at league-average. implied_OAA_season = implied_delta_per_BIP × innings × **2.2 BIP/inning** (constant; sensitivity ±30% noted — affects slope, not correlation).
- **Data/split**: all `fielding_profile` rows (2016–2025 seasons present), positions 2B/SS/3B (IF group), LF/CF/RF (OF group); 1B reported as diagnostic only (OAA at 1B dominated by non-range skills). Rate-standardized: correlate implied_delta_per_BIP vs OAA/innings.

Secondary = pooled OLS slope of actual_OAA_season on implied_OAA_season (magnitude check). Guardrails: per-position correlations, n per group, Pearson vs Spearman agreement.

Slope ∈ **[0.2, 0.8]** — expect the engine to OVER-credit speed differences (all range variation attributed to speed).
- **Decision rule (pre-registered, resolves C)**: **accept & stop** iff both groups' Spearman CIs exclude 0 AND pooled slope ∈ [0.5, 2.0]. **Re-derivation warranted** iff any group's CI includes 0 or slope outside [0.5, 2.0] — with the failing group scoping the work. - **Threats (pre-stated)**: OAA measures more than range (positioning, arm, hands — biases correlation DOWN, esp. IF; a low correlation is partly ceiling, not pure model failure); same-season sprint speed (fine for validation, not prediction); min-innings selection; BIP/inning constant (slope only); engine cross-coverage constants shared across players (mostly cancel in deltas).

OF reach model is retained as-is meanwhile.

## 2026-09-01 — P0: engine out-probability model vs. trivial zone baseline — RUN 2026-09-02

- **Status**: RUN. Pre-registered design (below) fixed in advance on 2026-09-01; harness built and executed 2026-09-02. Design fields unchanged post-hoc; results appended.
- **Hypothesis**: The engine's per-location coverage value, evaluated at the standard alignment, is a valid P(out) — i.e. it predicts realized out/hit on real batted balls **better than a plain 8-cell `fielding_zone` out-rate lookup** (baseline #1), and is approximately calibrated (reliability slope ≈ 1).
- **Decision it can change**: Whether the engine's `coverage`/`predicted_hit_pct` outputs can be trusted as probabilities at all — the gate to either (pass →) attempt the P1 counterfactual/prescriptive evaluation, or (fail →) relabel/fix the coverage model before any prescriptive claim. Also directly resolves the TRUST_REPORT P1 "`predicted_hit_pct` semantics mislabel."
- **Scope / what it does NOT test**: Validates **P(out | standard alignment)** only. It does **not** validate the prescriptive `oaa_delta` (the recommended-alignment leg is an unobserved counterfactual). Necessary, not sufficient. A pass does not prove the recommendations help; a fail proves they cannot be trusted.
- **Estimand**: For one in-play batted ball *i* landing at (xᵢ, yᵢ) with trajectory tᵢ fielded from a standard alignment: predicted `p̂ᵢ` = engine combined coverage at (xᵢ, yᵢ) using the trajectory-appropriate infield/outfield weighting from `_expected_outs` (ground → IF cov + 0.15×OF; air → OF cov + 0.30×IF) with **league-average fielders at `STANDARD_POSITIONS`**; target `yᵢ` = 1 if converted to an out. Engine prediction is batter-independent → **no point-in-time exposure on the engine side**; only baselines are fit and thus split-controlled.
- **Baseline ladder**: (0) global constant out rate [calibration floor, AUC≡0.5]; **(1) out rate by `fielding_zone` 1–8 — THE BAR**; (2) out rate by zone × trajectory.
- **Data / split**:
  - `pitch_appearance`, in-play batted balls: `general_result IN ('hit','out')`, `hc_x/hc_y NOT NULL`, `ball_trajectory NOT NULL`; **exclude HR** (`specific_result='hr'`) in v0.
  - **Standard-alignment filter (Option A, chosen)**: ingest `if_fielding_alignment` / `of_fielding_alignment` from the raw Statcast pull (2 new nullable string cols + Alembic migration + backfill). Filter per trajectory: ground/line-in-infield → `if_fielding_alignment='Standard'`; air/fly → `of_fielding_alignment='Standard'`. *Build note:* verify ingest upserts (not insert-only) so a backfill re-pull populates the new columns on existing rows; else backfill via targeted UPDATE keyed on `mlb_play_id`.

Per-fold (per-season) log-loss deltas form the primary uncertainty view; within-fold uncertainty via **game-clustered bootstrap**. LOSO will also surface pre/post-2023 shift-ban regime instability — report per-season, do not average it away.
- **Metric (primary + guardrails)**: primary = **log loss**, engine vs. baseline #1 on identical test balls. Guardrails = Brier, AUC (discrimination), calibration curve + slope/intercept (probability validity). Report engine−baseline log-loss delta per season fold with game-clustered bootstrap CI.
- **Expected effect (written in advance)**: If the coverage geometry carries real spatial out-information beyond coarse zones, engine log loss < baseline #1 with CI separation in most folds, and calibration slope in ~[0.8, 1.2]. Plausible null: engine ≈ baseline #1 (coarse 8-zone weighting + "rough" coordinate transform leave little residual signal). Failure mode to watch: engine well-discriminating (AUC > baseline) but miscalibrated (slope ≠ 1) → `coverage` orders correctly but isn't a probability → relabel, don't discard.
- **Decision rule (pre-registered)**:
